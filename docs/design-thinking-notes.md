# Design Thinking Notes

## Requested solution
"Build us an AI chatbot."

## Better human-centered question
How can we help a student find trustworthy guidance and reach the right person without repeating the whole story?

## Three possible solution directions
1. Better search and navigation.
2. RAG assistant that always shows approved sources.
3. Guided handoff workflow that carries only necessary context.

## What I would investigate next
Write one question you would ask a real student before choosing a solution.
