# Intent: CampusConnect

Author: <your name>
Status: draft

## Problem
Students can encounter conflicting or outdated campus guidance and may not know which office owns the answer.

## Proposed outcome
Help a student identify a trustworthy next step, see the source, and reach a human when confidence is low.

## Affected users
Students, advisors, service-office staff, and content owners.

## Constraints
Use approved or synthetic information only. Do not store private student data.

## Open questions
- Which student questions create the most confusion?
- Which sources are authoritative?
- When should the system stop and hand off to a person?
