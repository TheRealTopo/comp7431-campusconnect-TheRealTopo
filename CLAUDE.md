## Working rules
- Use approved or synthetic information only.
- Plan before proposing code.
- Show the source and content owner.
- Use a safe human handoff when confidence is low.

## Planned commands
- Run: python app.py
- Test: pytest

## Known traps
- Do not invent office ownership.
- Do not place private student data or secrets in this repository.
- Do not change a test merely to hide a product failure.
